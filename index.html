🎓 Purdue apartment lottery (2027–28): key steps & dates
Opt-in window opens tomorrow: Mon Sept 28, 2026, 9 a.m. ET, and closes Sun Oct 4, 11:59 p.m. ET. Miss it and there's no second chance this fall.

Who can enter: only students who currently live in Purdue University Residences (a dorm or Purdue apartment). Students living off campus can't enter; they can join a waitlist starting January 2027. Undergrads only.

  1. Opt in (Sept 28 – Oct 4, 2026) in the Housing Portal: sign the 2027–28 housing application, then either name up to 3 roommates (groups of up to 4) or choose "express placement" (Purdue assigns her; no roommates, ranks room type and price). Each roommate must opt in and sign their own application, and must accept the request.
  2. Results email by Oct 7, 2026 (to her Purdue email). It will say one of three things: (a) a time slot to pick a room, (b) a time slot to be assigned, or (c) not selected. Selection is random. A roommate group is picked all together or not at all, and groups are weighted so being in a group doesn't raise the odds.
  3. Room selection (6-hour online slot, one per group). Rising juniors/seniors pick first: Oct 20–22 (make-up day Oct 23). Current first-years pick Oct 27–29 (make-up day Oct 30). A first-year in a group with upperclassmen gets the upperclassmen slot. Only rooms left at her slot time are available. A group of 4 can pick a 4-bedroom; a group of 3 can pick a 3-person unit (e.g. 125 Waldron 3BR, Aspire 2BR-for-3). She must place her roommates into the unit during the slot.
  4. Contract becomes binding: Oct 26, 2026, 11:59 p.m. ET for upperclassmen (Nov 2 for first-years). She can cancel in the portal before then at no cost. After that, the returner cancellation fee is $250 (to July 31), $500 (Aug 1 to contract start), $750 (after start), per the 2026–27 fee table.

Cost: 2027–28 rates aren't approved yet; she'll see them during room selection. The map shows 2026–27 rates. A meal plan is required in every Purdue apartment except Hilltop. Everyone gets the 14-Track pre-assigned ($5,696 in 2026–27) and can switch in summer 2027. Apartment residents can drop to a block plan ($1,814–$2,524). Purdue lists a $200 non-refundable housing application fee; it doesn't say whether returning students pay it.

If she's not picked: she goes on a standby list automatically and may be contacted if space opens. Otherwise there's a waitlist in January 2027. Waitlist offers come with a deadline, and the contract binds 72 hours after signing. Private-lease tip: many private 2027–28 leases are already posting (see other pins). Try not to sign a private lease before the Oct 7 result, and don't pick a Purdue room if she's already signed a private lease. The Purdue contract binds Oct 26.

Note: 3rd and West is master-leased, and the reported lease only covers 2025–26 and 2026–27. Check that it's on the 2027–28 room list.
